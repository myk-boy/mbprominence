Approach

A home should age with grace, not demand constant upkeep.

Real luxury is a clear structure and honest materials. Each house we design pairs refined space with practical self-sufficiency: passive solar orientation, natural ventilation and surfaces that grow richer over decades.

Honest materials

Locally quarried stone, untreated oak and lime plaster.

Quiet autonomy

Off-grid readiness, rainwater harvesting and precise thermal design.

Bespoke process

From the first land survey to the last piece of joinery.
