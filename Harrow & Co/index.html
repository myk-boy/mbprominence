04 / INSIGHTS

Short notes on how we think.

I-03
Corporate & M&ANovember 2026 · 4 min read

What to settle before a minority investment term sheet

Agree the exit before the price. Valuation attracts the attention, but drag-along and tag-along rights, pre-emption and a realistic route to liquidity decide what a minority stake is actually worth.

Set out information rights and reserved matters in plain language at term-sheet stage. Disputes later usually trace back to something that was assumed rather than written.

I-02
Capital & Family GovernanceSeptember 2026 · 5 min read

Succession is a governance question before it is a tax one

Decide who decides. Families that document how decisions are made, and who may vote, sell or step in, tend to find the tax and structuring work far simpler.

Cross-border families should map where each member is resident and where assets sit before choosing a holding structure. Specialist tax advice in each country belongs early in the process, not at the end.

I-01
Commercial & DisputesJuly 2026 · 3 min read

Pre-litigation: how to keep a commercial dispute commercial

Fix the record early. A short, factual chronology and a clear statement of the outcome you want will do more for a negotiation than an aggressive opening letter.

Check the contract’s notice, escalation and governing-law clauses first. They often set the order of play and the cost of leaving it.
